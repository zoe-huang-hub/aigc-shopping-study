About roast level (Agtron): The Agtron number is a standard measurement of how darkly coffee beans are roasted. Lower numbers mean darker roasts and higher numbers mean lighter roasts; most coffees fall between about 45 (dark) and 75 (light). In general, darker roasts taste less acidic, with a smoother and fuller body, while lighter roasts taste brighter and more acidic, with a lighter body.
