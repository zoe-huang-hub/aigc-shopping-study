About noise cancellation (ANC): Active noise cancellation (ANC) is measured in decibels (dB) of outside noise removed. Higher numbers mean more noise is blocked; most wireless earbuds fall between about 25 dB (moderate) and 45 dB (strong). In general, higher ratings reduce steady background noise such as trains, airplanes, and air conditioning more effectively, while lower ratings leave more of that noise audible.
